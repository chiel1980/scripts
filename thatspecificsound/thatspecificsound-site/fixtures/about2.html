About

A bit more background on why I started this website — so here goes:

My name is Michiel van Es. I’m an avid guitar player and hobbyist with a lifelong love for hardcore and metal bands from the 90s up to today, especially Earth Crisis.

My hope is that this website will serve as an archive for anyone who shares a passion for heavy guitar sounds.
If you have any questions or feedback, feel free to reach out through one of my other personal websites or shoot me an email at mve@thatspecificsound.nl .
